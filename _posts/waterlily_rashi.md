# Water Lily (Source: Wikipedia)

_Nymphaea_ (/nɪmˈfiːə/) is a genus of hardy and tender aquatic plants in the family Nymphaeaceae. The genus has a cosmopolitan distribution. Many species are cultivated as ornamental plants, and many cultivars have been bred. Some taxa occur as introduced species where they are not native, and some are weeds.[1] Plants of the genus are known commonly as water lilies, or [waterlilies](Q16029052) in the United Kingdom.
