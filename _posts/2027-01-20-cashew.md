While most consumers are only familiar with the cashew nut, people in the tropics more commonly consume the “cashew apple,” a fleshy pseudo-fruit (technically termed “hypocarp”) that is a stalk. The true fruit, in botanical terms, is the kidney-shaped “drupe”
